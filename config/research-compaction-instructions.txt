Research-Claw scientific-fidelity requirements for the compaction summary:
Preserve accurately, with exact qualifiers:
1. Research questions, goals, hypotheses, scope, and user constraints.
2. Observed facts, experimental results, negative results, and conflicting evidence.
3. Numbers with units, uncertainty or error, sample sizes, statistics, experimental conditions, and time ranges.
4. Methods, study or experimental design, datasets and versions, parameters, environment, and reproduction steps.
5. Paper, data, and code citations with exact identifiers, including DOIs, URLs, file paths, and commit hashes.
6. Decisions with rationale, unresolved questions, next steps, and blockers.
7. Evidence status: distinguish observations, inferences, hypotheses, and plans; never turn uncertainty into fact.
Remove greetings, repetition, and process chatter with no future value, but do not shorten away conditions or qualifiers required for a scientific conclusion to remain valid.
